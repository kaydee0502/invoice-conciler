# Dispute: ALPINE-0726 volume discount not applied

**What happened.** BlueFin tendered 40 consignments to Alpine Express in July 2026, which qualifies for the contract's volume discount. Invoice ALPINE-0726 applies no discount: the printed adjustment is ₹0.00.

**What the contract says.** Clause 5: "In any calendar month in which the Customer tenders **more than 12 consignments**, the Carrier will apply a **5% discount to the invoice total** for that month's invoice" (alpine-express.md §5).

**The numbers.** Adjustment on the invoice: ₹0.00. Adjustment due under the contract on the lines whose amount we could determine: a discount of ₹1,007.46. Difference owed to BlueFin: ₹1,007.46. This is a lower bound. Line AE-3005 (ALPINE-0726#5) has no determinable contract amount and has been escalated separately. The discount on that line will be settled together with it once its rate is decided.

**Action.** Ask Alpine Express for a credit note for ₹1,007.46 against invoice ALPINE-0726 for the July volume discount under §5. Tell them that the discount on consignment AE-3005 is still open and will be added once that line's rate is agreed.

**Disposition:** dispute.
