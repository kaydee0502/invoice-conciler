# Dispute: ALPINE-0726 volume discount not applied

**What happened:** BlueFin tendered 40 consignments to Alpine Express in July 2026, which qualifies for the contract's volume discount, but invoice ALPINE-0726 applies no discount at all.

**What the contract says:** Clause 5 (alpine-express.md §5): "In any calendar month in which the Customer tenders **more than 12 consignments**, the Carrier will apply a **5% discount to the invoice total** for that month's invoice." Forty consignments is well above that threshold.

**The numbers:**
- Discount printed on the invoice: ₹0.00
- Discount owed on the lines with a determinable contract amount: ₹1,007.46
- Difference owed to BlueFin: ₹1,007.46

This is a lower bound. Consignment AE-3005 on the same invoice is escalated separately because its rate is undetermined; the discount on that line will be settled together with that escalation.

**Action:** Ask Alpine Express to issue a credit note for ₹1,007.46 against invoice ALPINE-0726 for the unapplied volume discount, and to confirm that the discount will be applied to the AE-3005 line once its rate is agreed.

**Disposition:** dispute.
