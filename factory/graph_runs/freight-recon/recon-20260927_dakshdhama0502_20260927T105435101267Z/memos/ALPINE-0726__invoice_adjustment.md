# Dispute: ALPINE-0726 missing volume discount

**What happened:** BlueFin tendered 40 consignments to Alpine Express in July 2026, which qualifies for the contract's volume discount. Invoice ALPINE-0726 does not apply any discount; its printed adjustment is ₹0.00.

**What the contract says:** Clause 5: "In any calendar month in which the Customer tenders **more than 12 consignments**, the Carrier will apply a **5% discount to the invoice total** for that month's invoice" (alpine-express.md §5).

**The numbers:** Printed adjustment ₹0.00. The contract discount on the lines with a determinable contract amount is ₹1,007.46. That ₹1,007.46 is owed to BlueFin. It is a minimum: it excludes line ALPINE-0726#5 (consignment AE-3005), which is escalated because its rate is undetermined.

**Action:** Ask Alpine Express to issue a credit note for ₹1,007.46 against invoice ALPINE-0726 for the Clause 5 volume discount. Tell them that the discount on consignment AE-3005 will be settled separately, once the rate for that line is agreed.

**Disposition:** dispute. The ₹1,007.46 discount is due under the contract and was not applied.
